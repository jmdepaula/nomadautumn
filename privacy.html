Privacy policy

Neighbourhood puzzle game by Nomadautumn (Android, com.nomadautumn.match3).
Last updated: 23 November 2026.

In short

  • We do not collect personal data. We (Nomadautumn) receive nothing about what happens in the game.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no usage analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not read the advertising ID, location, contacts, photos or other device identifiers.
  • Progress stays on the device. It only goes to the cloud if the player connects Google Play Games (see below).

What is stored on the device

So the game can continue where it left off, we store on the device itself:

  • progress: level reached, best score, a game in progress, the parts of the neighbourhood already fixed, and achievements;
  • lives and today's play time;
  • the chosen language;
  • if family mode is on: the rules the adult chose (lives and minutes per day), the tasks the adult wrote and the PIN. The PIN is never stored as typed: only a hashed version (PBKDF2 with salt) is kept, from which the PIN cannot be recovered;
  • that the age question has been answered. Neither the age nor the year of birth is stored: the answer is only used, at that moment, to turn on family mode for players aged 12 or under.

We do not send this data anywhere, except through the Google Play Games connection (below). If Android backup is turned on for the device, Android itself may include it in the user's Google account backup, to restore it on a new phone; that is managed by Google and the device settings. On the device, it is deleted when the game is uninstalled or when the app's data is cleared in the Android settings.

Google Play Games (optional)

The game can connect to Google Play Games, so progress can continue on another device and achievements can be shown. It is optional: without it, the game works the same way.

When connected, the game stores in the player's Google account, through Google Play Games services, a copy of what is described above (progress, lives and today's play time, achievements and, if it is on, family mode with the hashed PIN and the tasks). That data is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it.

For children under 13, connecting needs an adult: the game asks for the family mode PIN and, on a Google account supervised with Family Link, the Google Play Games profile needs parental approval.

To delete this data: in the Google Play Games app settings you can delete a game's data or the whole profile. You can also write to us (contact below) and we will explain how.

Children

The game is for all ages, including children. It is designed not to collect personal data from anyone and follows the Google Play Families policies. Family mode lets an adult limit play time and lives, with a PIN.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play, which may collect data about the installation and the device under Google's privacy policy. That is outside our control.

Your rights

You can ask us about your data or for help deleting it at any time. Since we receive no data from the game, in most cases the answer is to explain where it is (on the device or in the Google account) and how to delete it.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page first, with the new date at the top.
